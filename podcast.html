      Licence log

      Every piece of music, logged once, with proof of its licence on the day you took it.

        Where to look

        • Free Music ArchiveFilter by licence. Strong on indie, folk and electronic.
        • ccMixterRemixes and vocals. Lots of BY-NC; check each one.
        • JamendoBig catalogue. Use the free listening licences, not its paid ones.
        • Internet Archive netlabelsWhole albums from small labels, mostly CC.
        • Bandcamp, tagged Creative CommonsCheck the licence line at the foot of each album page.
        • OpenverseSearches several archives at once, filtered by licence.

        Or ask the artist. Small artists often say yes, even for an NC or ND song. Alfred will draft the email.

        The licences, briefly
        BY
        Credit the artist: title, artist, source link, licence and its link, and say if you changed it.
        SA
        Share-alike. If you talk over it, trim it or mix it, the episode must go out under the same licence.
        NC
        Non-commercial. No ads, sponsors or paid tiers on any episode that uses it.
        ND
        No derivatives. Play it whole and untouched, or not at all. Fades and beds count as changes.
        CC0
        No conditions. A credit is still good manners.
        Proof
        Licences can change after you download. Keep a screenshot or an archived link of the page as it was.
