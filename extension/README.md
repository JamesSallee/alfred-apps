# Send to Alfred (Chrome extension)

Sends the page you're on, or a link, to the Reading Room. One tab at a time.

- **Click the button** in Chrome's toolbar: the page goes on the shelf, with its reading time.
- **Right-click a page or a link** for "Send to Alfred", or "Send to Alfred's cabinet of curiosities" for something to look into rather than read.

It keeps no sign-in or data of its own: it opens the Reading Room in a small window, which shelves the link
and closes itself. If that window asks you to sign in, do it once and it remembers.

## Installing (once)

1. Download this repository (Code → Download ZIP) and unzip it, or use a clone.
2. In Chrome, go to `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this `extension` folder.
4. Pin it: the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar → the pin beside "Send to Alfred".

To update it later, replace the folder and click the reload arrow on its card in `chrome://extensions`.
